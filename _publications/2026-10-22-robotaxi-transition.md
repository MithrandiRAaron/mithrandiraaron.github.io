This paper studies the dynamic transition from human-driven ride-hailing services to robotaxis. The platform subsidizes human drivers while using data collected from their trips to train its full self-driving (FSD) system. We examine whether robotaxis eventually replace human drivers entirely and how the platform's data-collection policy influences the transition.
