**Status:** In preparation for submission.

**Presentation:** Scheduled talk at the DSI 2026 conference.

We study how creator portfolios are formed in digital marketplaces and the resulting production challenges faced by platforms. Because creators make assortment decisions while platforms determine production and fulfillment strategies, decentralized incentives can lead to misalignment. We examine how marketplace governance mechanisms can reshape creator portfolios, improve coordination with hybrid fulfillment, and enhance platform performance.
